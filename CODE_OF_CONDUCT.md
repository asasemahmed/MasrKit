# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We pledge to make participation in MasrKit a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of background, identity, experience, or personal characteristics. We value curiosity, evidence, respectful disagreement, and the diversity of Egyptian users and Arabic-speaking communities.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful, specific, and constructive.
- Challenge ideas with evidence; do not attack people.
- Avoid stereotypes, gatekeeping, harassment, sexualized attention, threats, and disclosure of private information.
- Accept correction and focus on what improves the project and community.

## Enforcement

Report conduct issues privately to the project maintainers using the security/contact channel listed by the repository owner. Maintainers will protect reporter privacy as far as practical, review reports fairly, and may edit or remove contributions, warn participants, temporarily restrict participation, or permanently ban participants according to severity and pattern.

This policy applies in project spaces and when someone publicly represents the project. Maintainers are responsible for consistent enforcement and must recuse themselves when they have a conflict.

This code is inspired by the Contributor Covenant 2.1 and is provided here in a concise project-specific form.
