# Egyptian ecommerce product

```text
Use MasrKit's arabic-ui, arabic-rtl, egyptian-copy, egyptian-product-ux, and egyptian-backend skills.

Design an Arabic-first product page and checkout for an Egyptian home-goods shop. Format prices in EGP, ask for Egyptian address components progressively, keep phone input readable, and show delivery/payment choices only when confirmed by backend capability. Include stock, retry, payment-pending, and order-confirmed states. Do not assume cash on delivery is available.
```
